    How to play
    • Shop. Five units each round. Three copies of a unit merge into a stronger one.
    • Economy. Banked gold pays interest: 1 per 10 gold, up to 5 a round. Streaks pay extra.
    • Board. One unit per level. The front rows trade smaller and forgive part of a drawdown: the place for what swings. The back rows trade bigger, and a back unit with nobody in front of it in its column pays extra for every drawdown.
    • Tickers. A unit's column picks which symbol of its market it trades. Spread to diversify.
    • Items. Two components make an item: tighter stops, wider targets, bigger size, drawdown shields.
    • Fight. Every unit trades its class's strategy on the round's slice. A good trade grows the unit's size, a bad one shrinks it, a disaster wipes it out. The bigger board wins.
